## Overview

**Angels Tapayan POS** is a business management and Point-of-Sale (POS) system I designed and developed to help manage the day-to-day operations of a water-refilling business.

The system was designed to centralize sales, inventory, customer management, credit and collection tracking, deliveries, expenses, employee attendance, commissions, reporting, and business analytics into a single application.

The project focuses on reducing manual record-keeping and providing business owners with a clearer view of daily operations and financial performance.

---

## Business Problem

Small businesses often rely on manual records, spreadsheets, notebooks, and separate processes for managing daily operations.

The system was designed to address challenges such as:

- Manual sales recording
- Difficulty tracking customer balances
- Manual inventory monitoring
- Difficulty tracking gallon/container movement
- Manual delivery records
- Separate expense records
- Difficulty monitoring employee attendance
- Manual commission calculations
- Limited visibility of business performance
- Time-consuming report preparation
